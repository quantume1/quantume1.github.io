What I do

Everything here I use every week. Nothing on this list is from a syllabus.

Automating the boring parts

Python that takes over repetitive work. I plug into APIs, handle the rate limits, catch the errors, and make sure a job that dies at 3am picks up where it stopped instead of starting over. Selenium when there's no API.

Keeping data clean

MySQL: designing the schema, normalizing it, writing the queries. And Excel for real, the messy kind. Big files, broken encodings, pivots, reconciling two lists that should match and don't.

Making sites fast

I write the pages by hand in HTML, CSS and Liquid, including the emails customers get. Then the part nobody enjoys: finding the tracking script loaded twice, and cutting what doesn't need to be there.

Getting people to show up

SEO, the technical side and the writing side. Email campaigns from the template to the send calendar. Google Analytics 4 and Merchant Center, including the product feeds that break for reasons nobody documents.

Running the thing

Planning, priorities, deadlines, suppliers, costs. Plus the calls nobody wants: a late shipment, a payment dispute, an unhappy customer. I handle those to the end, in whatever language they came in.

Three languages

French, English and Arabic. I work in all three, not just order coffee in them.
